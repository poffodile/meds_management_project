# Care One OS — Working Brand Guidelines

> Built from supplied logo assets and colour references (owner-provided, 2026-06-23).
> Source: brand guidelines sheet (image #84). This is the source of truth for brand colours, type and logo usage.

## 1. Logo system
- **Primary logo** — full-colour wordmark. Use on **white or very light** backgrounds.
- **Reverse logo** — **white** wordmark. Use on **dark backgrounds only** (navy or black).
- **Icon / device mark** — the coloured ring (no wordmark). For **app icon, favicon, social avatar**.

## 3. Typography and scale
- **Supporting typeface:** Montserrat / Avenir Next / a clean geometric sans. (The exact logo typeface can't be confirmed from the raster files — use a clean geometric sans for all supporting comms.)
- Headings **bold**, body **regular**.

| Level | Size / weight | Use |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | 56–72 px / Bold | Primary headlines |
| H2 | 32–40 px / Bold | Section headings |
| H3 | 22–28 px / Bold | Cards and callouts |
| Body | 14–18 px / Regular | Main copy and supporting text |
| Caption | 12–14 px / Regular | Small labels and footnotes |

## 4. Sizing, spacing and usage
- **Logo clear space:** keep at least the width of one coloured arc end around the full logo.
- **Minimum print size:** full logo 35 mm wide; icon 10 mm wide.
- **Minimum digital size:** full logo 180 px wide; icon 32 px wide.
- **Backgrounds:** full-colour logo on white or very light neutral backgrounds.
- **Reverse usage:** white logo on **navy or black only**. Do **not** place the black logo on dark backgrounds.
- **Do not:** stretch, rotate, recolour, or separate the ring segments from the wordmark.
